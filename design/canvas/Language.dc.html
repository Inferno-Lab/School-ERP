Nest, the language of EduNest admin and console

Calm, warm and a little bit alive.

One system, three surfaces: school admin, the console and every empty or error moment. Soft cards on a lilac floor, one ink for the actions that matter, and a small owl called Nesti for the human moments.

Every colour has a soft tint, a deep tone for text on the tint, and a dark-mode twin. Text on colour passes 4.5:1. Red is kept for errors and deletion, never decoration.

Cards 28, inner wells 18, buttons and chips are pills. A 4 px grid; cards breathe with 26 px inside and 20 px between. Shadows are tinted violet, never grey.

Motion, press an easing

Expo out, entrances
Spring, presses
Linear, never used

Enter in 600 to 900 ms, leave in 200. Lists rise in a stagger. Everything respects reduced motion.

Icons, one stroke

1.7 px stroke, round caps, 24 px grid. Colour comes from the text, so icons never fight the page.

Charts, our own grammar

Pencil columns

Trends over days. Today is the solid one.

Roll dots

One dot is one child. Present, late, absent.

Fee jars

Money filling up, wave on top.

Charts are drawn from school objects, not from a chart library: pencils, children and jars. Axes are quiet, values appear on touch.

Nesti, who and when

Only where a person would speak.

Welcome, empty, notice, coin, book, lost and party. Never in tables, never in errors that block money. Head-shake on a wrong password.

Rules of the house

  • 1One ink. One dark pill action per screen; everything else is quiet.
  • 2Wrong should wobble. Errors vibrate once, say what happened and how to fix it.
  • 3Nothing is blank. Empty means Nesti, one honest sentence and the next step.
  • 4Menus grow from their button. Overlays come from where you touched.
  • 5Light and dark are equals. Every token has a twin; dark is not an afterthought.
  • 6Keyboard first. Ctrl K finds anything; focus rings are violet and always visible.
  • 7The console is the same family. Darker by default, same cards, same Nesti.
  • 8Chalk and Glass stays in the app. Admin and console never borrow it.
